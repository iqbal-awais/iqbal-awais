# Awais Iqbal
 
Postdoctoral Researcher, College of Ecology, Lanzhou University, Lanzhou, China
 
I study how soil and root-associated microbial communities reorganize under disturbance, and what this means for carbon and nutrient cycling. My work on the Qinghai–Tibetan Plateau covers bacteria, fungi, and archaea in bulk soil, rhizosphere, and rhizoplane, their cross-domain interaction networks, and the link between network structure and ecosystem multifunctionality.
 
These repositories hold the R code and processed data behind my published papers, so that others can check and reuse the analyses.
 
## Research areas
 
- Alpine wetland and grassland microbial ecology
- Rhizosphere and rhizoplane microbiology
- Cross-domain (bacteria–fungi–archaea) co-occurrence networks
- Ecosystem multifunctionality and soil function
 
## Methods used in these repositories
 
- Amplicon processing (16S rRNA, ITS and archaeal markers) with QIIME 2
- Community ecology in R: vegan, phyloseq, mixed models, random forest
- Co-occurrence networks, keystone taxa, robustness and vulnerability (igraph, Gephi)
- Multifunctionality indices and structural equation modelling
